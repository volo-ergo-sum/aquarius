An unofficial, single-site GeckoView wrapper around gemini.google.com. One activity, one GeckoSession, one site — Mozilla's engine, its own cookie jar, no address bar and no tabs. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google.

The stock Gemini app is 5.0 MiB, declares three permissions and no INTERNET, and launching it brings a com.google.android.googlequicksearchbox activity to the foreground: it is a launcher for the Google app. On a Pixel 9a running Android 17, with that package disabled and no GSA process left running, Aquarius signed in and rendered Gemini in 215 ms.

That tests independence from Google's apps, not from Play Services, which was installed and enabled throughout. Whether this runs on a device without Play Services is untested.

It is not lighter and it is not faster. Measured at the same moment, it uses about 12% more PSS and 32% more RSS than the pair it replaces, because GeckoView maps a 145 MiB libxul.so into each content process, and the APK is 195.9 MiB. What it buys is independence.

Gemini Live does not exist in the web app, so this cannot provide it. There is no share target, it does not take the assistant role, passkey sign-in is not wired up, and Workspace SSO is untested. arm64-v8a only.
