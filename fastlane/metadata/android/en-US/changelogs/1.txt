First release.

Opens gemini.google.com/app in an embedded GeckoView. Voice input, image
and file attachments, downloads. The session lives in a Gecko profile
inside the app's own sandbox and is excluded from Android backup.

Verified on a Pixel 9a running Android 17: cold start 215 ms with
com.google.android.googlequicksearchbox disabled.

arm64-v8a only.
